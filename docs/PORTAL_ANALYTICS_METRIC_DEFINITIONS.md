# PORTAL-001.3 — Analytics metric definitions and status

All indicators require a declared publication/snapshot ID, cohort, and date basis. Missing coverage must remain distinguishable from zero. Statuses below are **ПОДТВЕРЖДЕНО**, **ЧАСТИЧНО ПОДТВЕРЖДЕНО**, or **НЕ ПОДТВЕРЖДЕНО**.

| Metric | Candidate source / grain | Definition needed | Current status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total projects | `proj.i`; optionally `projects_current.project_id` | Count distinct projects under an approved active/archive/date/name-cohort rule; include zero-claim projects. | **ЧАСТИЧНО** — 212 of 213 strict October-name candidates exist in `proj`. Active/archive semantics not decoded. The DataLens claim-summary sheet contains 495 project IDs, not a demonstrated all-project denominator. |
| Total visits | MariaDB `visit`, composite `(proj,i)` | Count distinct project/visit keys. Apply a date field only after its business meaning and timezone are validated. | **ЧАСТИЧНО** — visit rows and keys match in sampled SQL records; 8,206 SQL visit rows in the current strict 213-ID cohort. Across 207 Portal-collected projects SQL has 8,197 keys vs 8,157 Portal keys. Dates do not reconcile fully. |
| Action/report records | `my.i`, linked through `my.visit → visit.i`; Portal `action_id` | Count distinct `(project_id, action_id)` after confirming which `my` states constitute a report/action and what “reviewed” means. | **ЧАСТИЧНО** — all Portal action IDs matched `my.i` in four control projects; cohort totals differ by 51 IDs (8,070 Portal vs 8,121 SQL in 207 projects). ## Calculation rules before publication

1. Retain `(project_id,visit_id)` as the analytical visit key even though the selected SQL samples had distinct visit IDs.
2. Keep Portal fact rows, action IDs, SQL `my.i` rows, visit keys, raw `claim.i` rows, log-derived episode IDs, and claim visit keys as separate grains.
3. Prefer `visit.proj` for project attribution only under an approved rule; preserve and quality-flag the five observed claim/visit project mismatches rather than silently repairing them.
4. Keep `visit.dt`, `visit.dt1`, `my.dt`, `my.dt_visit`, `claim.dt`, `claim.repdt`, and log event `dt` separate until their meanings and timezone are confirmed.
5. Preserve NULL/NOT_AVAILABLE for absent denominators, incomplete snapshots, and unclassified records. Do not turn unavailable coverage into zero.
